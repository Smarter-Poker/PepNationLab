# Peptide Popular Nicknames And Community Descriptions

> Research Use Only. This reference documents colloquial community names, shorthand,
> and "known-as" descriptors used in biohacking and research forums for each compound
> sold individually on PepNationLab. These are informal descriptors -- not clinical claims.
>
> Stacks excluded. Reconstitution supplies excluded.
> Last compiled: 2026-06-09

## Anti-Aging And Longevity

### Epithalon
- **Popular Names:** "The Immortality Peptide" / "The Telomere Peptide" / "The Russian Anti-Aging Secret"
- **Why:** Russian scientist Vladimir Khavinson developed it from pineal gland extracts.
  Community fascination centers on its reported telomerase activation and telomere
  elongation -- the mechanism most associated with biological aging reversal.

### FOXO4-DRI
- **Popular Names:** "The Zombie Cell Killer" / "The Senolytic Peptide" / "The Science Fiction Peptide"
- **Why:** Targets senescent cells (colloquially called "zombie cells" by biohackers) and
  triggers their selective self-destruction. The 2017 Cell paper showing restored fur and
  fitness in aged mice lit the community on fire.

### Glutathione
- **Popular Names:** "The Master Antioxidant" / "The Mother Of All Antioxidants" / "GSH"
- **Why:** The most abundant endogenous antioxidant and the one every other antioxidant
  system ultimately depends on to regenerate. "Master antioxidant" is used so consistently
  it is essentially a formal nickname in research circles.

### Melatonin
- **Popular Names:** "The Sleep Hormone" / "Nature's Sleeping Pill" / "The Darkness Hormone"
- **Why:** Produced naturally only in darkness by the pineal gland. The world's most
  recognizable sleep compound -- sold OTC in the US, prescription-only in most of Europe.

### NAD+
- **Popular Names:** "The Anti-Aging Molecule" / "The Fountain Of Youth IV" / "The Energy Currency Drip"
- **Why:** David Sinclair's work popularized NAD+ as the central driver of aging and
  sirtuin activity. IV NAD+ clinics market it as the anti-aging infusion, and the
  biohacking community treats it as the canonical longevity molecule.

### Pinealon
- **Popular Names:** "The Brain Shield" / "The Neuroprotective Russian Peptide" / "EDR"
- **Why:** Derived from pineal gland extracts in the Khavinson lineage. The community
  knows it primarily as a neuroprotective compound for oxidative stress and neuronal aging
  with a near-cult following in Eastern European longevity research circles.

### SS-31
- **Popular Names:** "The Mitochondria Rescue Peptide" / "Elamipretide" / "Bendavia"
- **Why:** Brand name Bendavia and INN elamipretide are used interchangeably with SS-31.
  The community describes it as the peptide that repairs mitochondrial membranes -- earned
  by its cardiolipin-binding mechanism and 2025 FDA approval for Barth syndrome.

### Thymalin
- **Popular Names:** "The Immune Clock Reset" / "The Thymus Restorer" / "The Soviet Immune Peptide"
- **Why:** Derived from calf thymus and developed in Soviet Russia. Research circles know
  it as the peptide that restores youthful T-cell populations in aged immune systems --
  essentially rewinding the immune clock.

### Thymosin Alpha-1
- **Popular Names:** "TA-1" / "The Immunity Peptide" / "Zadaxin" / "The T-Cell Booster"
- **Why:** Brand name Zadaxin is widely known. TA-1 is the universal shorthand. Approved
  in 30+ countries, it is the most clinically validated immunomodulatory peptide on the
  platform. The biohacking community calls it the gold standard for immune system support.

## Healing And Recovery

### BPC 157
- **Popular Names:** "God's Peptide" / "The Wolverine Peptide" / "The Gut Healer" / "The Body Protection Compound"
- **Why:** "God's Peptide" emerged from the sheer breadth of its healing research -- gut,
  tendon, ligament, nerve, bone, brain. "The Wolverine Peptide" (pre-dating the BPC+TB
  stack name) refers to Wolverine's regenerative ability. It is the most discussed
  research peptide online by a wide margin.

### KPV
- **Popular Names:** "The Anti-Inflammatory Tripeptide" / "The IBD Peptide" / "The Gut Soother"
- **Why:** Alpha-MSH derived fragment. Research community knows it primarily for gut
  inflammation and IBD models. Small, stable, highly targeted -- the gut-healing peptide
  for those who want something more focused than BPC 157.

### TB500 (Thymosin Beta-4)
- **Popular Names:** "The Injury Eraser" / "TB-4" / "The Athletic Recovery Peptide" / "The Healer"
- **Why:** WADA-banned for a reason -- athlete circles know it as the compound that
  accelerates recovery from injury and soft-tissue damage. Stacked constantly with BPC 157,
  earning the nickname "the other half of the Wolverine stack."

## Immunity And Wellness

### ARA290 (Cibinetide)
- **Popular Names:** "The Nerve Healer" / "The Neuropathy Peptide" / "The Pain Peptide"
- **Why:** Erythropoietin-derived fragment that hits the innate repair receptor without
  stimulating red blood cell production. Community knows it as the peptide for nerve pain
  and small-fiber neuropathy -- a quieter compound with a devoted following.

### B12
- **Popular Names:** "The Energy Shot" / "The Vitality Injection" / "The Red Shot"
- **Why:** The classic injectable B12 ampoule has been called "the energy shot" for
  decades in clinical and wellness settings. The bright red color of methylcobalamin
  solution gives it "The Red Shot" nickname in some circles.

### Cerebrolysin
- **Popular Names:** "The Brain Fuel" / "Russian Brain Juice" / "The Smart Drug Drip" / "The Nootropic IV"
- **Why:** A porcine-derived neurotrophic peptide mixture administered IV. Wildly popular
  in Russian and Eastern European neurological clinics. Western biohackers who have sourced
  it call it "Russian Brain Juice" -- it has near-mythical status in nootropic communities.

### DSIP
- **Popular Names:** "The Delta Sleep Peptide" / "The Stress-Sleep Peptide" / "The Cortisol Reducer"
- **Why:** Delta Sleep Inducing Peptide is its actual name -- the community shortened it
  to "the delta sleep peptide." It is known as much for its stress-hormone modulation as
  for sleep induction, earning the dual identity.

### LL37
- **Popular Names:** "Nature's Antibiotic" / "The Human Defensin" / "The Antimicrobial Peptide" / "The Innate Shield"
- **Why:** The only known human cathelicidin -- an endogenous antimicrobial peptide the
  body produces to defend against infection. Research circles call it "Nature's Antibiotic"
  because it kills bacteria, fungi, and viruses without the resistance concerns of
  conventional antibiotics.

### Selank
- **Popular Names:** "The Russian Xanax" / "Xanax Without The Addiction" / "The Anxiolytic Peptide"
- **Why:** Developed in Russia as an anxiolytic research compound. The community compares
  its calming profile to benzodiazepines but without dependency potential, making "Russian
  Xanax" the shorthand that stuck in every biohacking forum.

### Semax
- **Popular Names:** "Adderall In A Bottle" / "The Russian Nootropic" / "Brain Jet Fuel" / "The BDNF Peptide"
- **Why:** Russian ACTH analog known for intense cognitive enhancement, focus, and BDNF
  elevation. Called "Adderall In A Bottle" for its stimulant-like focus effects without
  being a stimulant. The flagship example of the genre.

### VIP (Vasoactive Intestinal Peptide)
- **Popular Names:** "The Mold Illness Peptide" / "The CIRS Peptide" / "The Brain Fog Eraser"
- **Why:** Dr. Ritchie Shoemaker's chronic inflammatory response syndrome (CIRS) protocol
  placed VIP at the center of mold illness treatment. The community knows it almost
  exclusively through that lens -- the peptide for biotoxin illness and brain fog from
  water-damaged buildings.

## Muscle Growth And Performance

### CJC-1295 With DAC
- **Popular Names:** "The Long-Acting GHRH" / "The Set-It-And-Forget-It Peptide" / "DAC CJC"
- **Why:** Drug Affinity Complex (DAC) extends its half-life to roughly 8 days, meaning
  once- or twice-weekly dosing in research protocols. Community calls it the "set it and
  forget it" growth hormone releaser for this reason.

### CJC-1295 Without DAC
- **Popular Names:** "Modified GRF 1-29" / "Mod GRF" / "The GH Pulse Peptide"
- **Why:** "Mod GRF 1-29" is the standard shorthand in the community -- distinguishing it
  from the DAC version. Stacked constantly with Ipamorelin to mimic the body's natural
  pulsatile GH release.

### Follistatin
- **Popular Names:** "The Muscle Multiplier" / "The Myostatin Blocker" / "The Freak Peptide"
- **Why:** Follistatin inhibits myostatin -- the protein that limits muscle growth. The
  research community calls it "the myostatin blocker" and biohackers use "the freak
  peptide" because myostatin-knockout animal models display extreme muscle hypertrophy.

### GHRP-2
- **Popular Names:** "The GH Releaser" / "The Hunger Peptide" / "The Ghrelin Mimic"
- **Why:** A potent ghrelin receptor agonist that stimulates GH release but also
  dramatically increases appetite. The community notes the hunger side effect as a
  distinguishing characteristic vs the cleaner Ipamorelin.

### GHRP-6
- **Popular Names:** "The Hunger Bomb" / "The Ravenous Peptide" / "GHRP-6 The Hungry"
- **Why:** The most appetite-stimulating GHRP. Community consensus: if GHRP-2 is hungry,
  GHRP-6 is ravenous. Research forums universally warn that GHRP-6 will make subjects eat
  constantly -- the hunger effect is the defining characteristic.

### Hexarelin
- **Popular Names:** "The Strongest GHRP" / "The Potent GH Stimulator" / "The Heart Peptide"
- **Why:** Most potent of the GHRPs for GH release. Also one of the only peptides with
  direct cardiac research outside of GH activity. Community knows it as the strongest
  tool in the GHRP class.

### HGH Fragment 176-191
- **Popular Names:** "HGH Frag" / "The Fat-Burning Fragment" / "The Lipolysis Peptide"
- **Why:** Isolated fat-burning C-terminal fragment of HGH without the growth-promoting
  effects of full HGH. "HGH Frag" is the universal shorthand -- the community values it
  as targeted lipolysis without insulin resistance.

### HMG (Human Menopausal Gonadotropin)
- **Popular Names:** "The Natural FSH" / "The Fertility Peptide" / "The Egg Stimulator"
- **Why:** Contains FSH and LH activity extracted from menopausal urine. Research
  communities refer to it as the natural gonadotropin alternative -- used in fertility
  research for ovarian stimulation and male gonadal axis support.

### IGF-1 LR3
- **Popular Names:** "Long R3" / "The Anabolic Signaler" / "The Muscle Cell Grower" / "Long-Acting IGF"
- **Why:** "Long R3" is the standard community shorthand for the LR3 variant. It is the
  downstream anabolic signal from GH -- the compound that tells muscle cells to grow and
  divide. Community treats it as the anabolic complement to GH axis peptides.

### Ipamorelin
- **Popular Names:** "The Clean GH Peptide" / "The Selective Releaser" / "The Gentle Giant Of GHRPs"
- **Why:** The most selective GHRP -- stimulates GH without meaningfully raising cortisol
  or prolactin, and with minimal hunger side effects vs GHRP-2 and GHRP-6. "Clean" is
  the word that defines its reputation in research forums.

### Sermorelin
- **Popular Names:** "The Natural GH Stimulator" / "The Anti-Aging GH Peptide" / "The Fountain Of Youth Peptide"
- **Why:** First-generation GHRH analog approved as a pharmaceutical before being
  discontinued as a drug. Now the most widely used compounded anti-aging GH peptide.
  Marketed heavily as a "natural" alternative to synthetic HGH.

### Tesamorelin
- **Popular Names:** "The Belly Fat Killer" / "The Visceral Fat Peptide" / "Egrifta"
- **Why:** Brand name Egrifta is FDA-approved for HIV-associated lipodystrophy --
  specifically visceral (belly) fat accumulation. Community repurposed this as "the
  belly fat peptide" for general visceral fat research. One of the few FDA-approved
  peptides on the platform.

## Sexual Health And Hormones

### HCG (Human Chorionic Gonadotropin)
- **Popular Names:** "The Test Booster" / "The PCT Hormone" / "The Natural Testosterone Starter" / "Pregnancy Hormone"
- **Why:** LH mimic used in post-cycle therapy (PCT) to restart endogenous testosterone
  production. Performance community knows it as the "test booster" and "PCT hormone."
  Clinically it is the pregnancy-detection hormone -- giving it dual nicknames in very
  different communities.

### KissPeptin-10
- **Popular Names:** "The Libido Master Switch" / "The Hormone Ignitor" / "The GnRH Trigger Peptide"
- **Why:** Kisspeptin activates GnRH neurons -- the upstream master switch for the entire
  reproductive hormone axis. Research community calls it the "master switch" because
  without kisspeptin signaling, the GnRH/LH/testosterone cascade does not fire.

### Oxytocin Acetate
- **Popular Names:** "The Love Hormone" / "The Bonding Hormone" / "The Trust Molecule" / "The Cuddle Chemical"
- **Why:** Paul Zak's research on oxytocin and trust/bonding gave the world "the trust
  molecule." "Love hormone" and "bonding hormone" are used interchangeably in virtually
  every popular science article ever written about it. Possibly the most culturally
  recognizable peptide nickname outside of Ozempic.

## Skin, Hair And Cosmetics

### AHK-CU (Alanine-Histidine-Lysine Copper)
- **Popular Names:** "The Hair Growth Copper Peptide" / "AHK" / "The Hair Follicle Peptide"
- **Why:** Distinguished from GHK-CU by the community as specifically hair-targeted. While
  GHK-CU does everything, AHK-CU is known as the copper peptide for hair follicle
  preservation and growth in research dermatology.

### GHK-CU (Glycine-Histidine-Lysine Copper)
- **Popular Names:** "Nature's Botox" / "The Blue Copper Peptide" / "The Wound Healing Copper Peptide" / "The Skin Rejuvenator"
- **Why:** "Nature's Botox" is the dominant community descriptor -- GHK-CU upregulates
  collagen, tightens skin, and improves wound healing without toxin injection. The "blue
  copper" nickname comes from the blue color of the copper-complexed peptide solution.
  One of the most widely researched cosmetic peptides.

### MT-1 (Melanotan 1)
- **Popular Names:** "The Tanning Peptide" / "The Sun-Free Tan" / "Afamelanotide" / "Scenesse"
- **Why:** Brand name Scenesse is FDA-approved for erythropoietic protoporphyria -- the
  sun-allergy disease. Community knows it as the melanocortin peptide that darkens skin
  pigmentation without UV exposure. Distinct from MT-2 in both mechanism and profile.

### SNAP-8
- **Popular Names:** "The Botox Alternative" / "The Wrinkle Eraser Peptide" / "The Anti-Wrinkle Octapeptide"
- **Why:** Acetyl-octapeptide that inhibits the SNARE complex -- the same mechanism Botox
  uses to reduce muscle contraction and expression lines, but topically. Cosmetic research
  community universally positions it as the peptide Botox alternative.

## Weight Loss And Metabolism

### 5-Amino-1MQ
- **Popular Names:** "The Fat Cell Killer" / "The NNMT Blocker" / "The Metabolic Switch" / "The Obesity Molecule Blocker"
- **Why:** Blocks NNMT (nicotinamide N-methyltransferase), an enzyme overexpressed in
  obese fat tissue that suppresses NAD+ and metabolism. Community calls it "the NNMT
  blocker" or "fat cell killer" because it targets the metabolic engine of fat cells
  specifically, not the appetite axis.

### AICAR (Acadesine)
- **Popular Names:** "The Exercise Pill" / "Lazy Man's Cardio" / "The AMPK Activator" / "Cardio In A Bottle"
- **Why:** The Narkar et al. 2008 Salk paper showing AICAR increased running endurance
  in sedentary mice by 44% with no exercise made every science headline. "Lazy Man's
  Cardio" and "Exercise In A Bottle" became the immediate community reaction. WADA banned
  it before any human trials completed.

### AOD9604
- **Popular Names:** "The Anti-Obesity Fragment" / "The Lipolysis Peptide" / "HGH Frag's Cousin" / "AOD"
- **Why:** C-terminal HGH fragment engineered specifically for fat metabolism without
  growth-stimulating effects. Positioned by the community as the fat-targeted companion
  to HGH Fragment 176-191, though different in sequence and mechanism.

### Cagrilintide
- **Popular Names:** "The Amylin Analog" / "The Appetite Off Switch" / "The GLP-1 Partner"
- **Why:** Long-acting amylin analog developed specifically to pair with semaglutide
  (the CagriSema combination). Community knows it as the complementary partner that
  handles the fullness/satiety side while semaglutide handles the appetite/GLP-1 side --
  described as flipping the appetite circuit off.

### MOTS-C
- **Popular Names:** "The Exercise Mimetic" / "The Mitochondrial Hormone" / "Exercise In A Shot"
- **Why:** Mitochondrial-encoded peptide that activates AMPK and AICAR-like pathways,
  improving insulin sensitivity and exercise capacity. Community groups it with AICAR
  as an "exercise mimetic" -- the mitochondrial version of cardio in a vial.

### Retatrutide
- **Popular Names:** "The Triple Threat" / "The Triple Agonist" / "GIP/GLP/Glucagon" / "The Next-Gen Obesity Drug"
- **Why:** Hits three receptors simultaneously: GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon. Phase 2 trials
  showed roughly 24% body weight reduction -- exceeding tirzepatide at the time. Community
  calls it "the triple threat" for the three-receptor mechanism and the most dramatic
  weight loss data seen in any clinical program.

### Semaglutide
- **Popular Names:** "Ozempic" / "The Hollywood Shot" / "The Miracle Weight Loss Drug" / "The Celebrity Drug"
- **Why:** Ozempic (diabetes brand) and Wegovy (obesity brand) are the most culturally
  dominant pharmaceutical brand names of the 2020s. "The Hollywood Shot" and "Celebrity
  Drug" emerged when widespread celebrity use and social media virality made semaglutide
  a cultural phenomenon beyond medicine.

### Survodutide
- **Popular Names:** "The Dual Agonist" / "GLP-1/Glucagon Dual" / "The Next Ozempic" / "Boehringer's GLP"
- **Why:** Dual GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist in Phase 3 for obesity. Community tracks
  it as the next major player after tirzepatide in the incretin wars. Named for its
  developer (Boehringer Ingelheim) in some circles and "the dual agonist" in others.

### Tirzepatide
- **Popular Names:** "Mounjaro" / "The Twincretin" / "Better Than Ozempic" / "The Dual Shot"
- **Why:** "Twincretin" is the scientific community's nickname for dual GIP/GLP-1 agonists.
  "Mounjaro" is the brand name that surpassed Ozempic in weight loss benchmarks. "Better
  Than Ozempic" became the community consensus after head-to-head weight loss comparisons
  showed superior outcomes.
