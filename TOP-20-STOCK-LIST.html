Buy Strategy HOW MUCH OF EACH

The 3-bucket first order

Bucket 1 — Volume engine (highest turn, highest risk): Tirzepatide + Semaglutide, in their lead sizes (Tirz 10/15/30, Sema 5/10). These will be a large share of your revenue. Size to your compliance comfort — they turn fast, so you won't sit on them, but they carry the enforcement risk in the Heat column. Retatrutide one tier lighter (investigational = highest legal exposure).

Bucket 2 — Safe core (stock DEEP, sleep easy): Wolverine, BPC-157, TB-500, GH Synergy, Glow, GHK-Cu, Ipamorelin, CJC-1295, Sermorelin, Tesamorelin. High demand, low regulatory heat — this is the backbone that keeps orders shipping same-day without legal worry.

Bucket 3 — Basket-fillers (LIGHT, a few units each): KLOW, Cagrilintide, MOTS-c, NAD+, PT-141, Semax/Selank, KPV, plus the Furnace/Lipolysis/Skinny-Shot blends. Don't overbuy — they complete carts and prevent "out of stock" but move slowly. KPV and BAC water/acetic acid are cheap, high-attach add-ons — never let those or bac water run out (every injectable order needs reconstitution supplies).

Don't forget the consumables

Every peptide order needs Bacteriostatic Water (you carry 3mL + 10mL) and some need Acetic Acid 0.6%. These are near-100% attach items and pennies to stock — running out of BAC water blocks a customer from using anything they bought. Stock these DEEP; they're the razor-blades to your razors.

Regulatory heat — size inventory accordingly

Highest heat (GLP-1 analogs): Semaglutide + Tirzepatide drew 50+ FDA warning letters to sellers in 2025; Retatrutide is investigational and cannot be compounded at all. The demand is real and so is enforcement — keep strict Research Use Only labeling, never imply human use, and size depth to your risk appetite rather than pure demand.

Elevated: BPC-157 (direct FDA warning-letter activity), PT-141, and any GLP-containing stack (Glow/KLOW contain BPC; CagriSema contains semaglutide).

Lower heat — your safe volume: TB-500, GHK-Cu, Ipamorelin, CJC-1295, Sermorelin (still compoundable), Tesamorelin, MOTS-c, Semax, Selank, Epithalon, NAD+. Lean into these for confident deep stocking and lead your marketing with the recovery/anti-aging story rather than the GLP-1 one.
