Module 1 - Complete Overview

What Exactly Is A Peptide?

A Peptide Is A Short Chain Of Amino Acids. Think Of Amino Acids As Individual Lego Bricks. A Peptide Is What You Get When You Connect 2 To 50 Of Those Bricks In A Specific Order. When Chains Grow Beyond 50, They Become Proteins.

The Three Key Properties Of Peptides

They Send Messages

Think Of A Peptide Like A Tiny Text Message For Your Cells. It Tells One Exact Cell What To Do, And Nothing Else Gets The Message.

They Do Not Last Long

Your Body Breaks Peptides Down Fast, In Hours Not Days. That Is A Good Thing. It Means Your Body Stays In Control.

Your Body Makes Them

Right Now, Your Body Is Making Peptides Of Its Own. Scientists Often Just Copy The Ones Your Body Already Knows How To Use.

Peptides You Already Know

Insulin

A Peptide That Controls Your Blood Sugar. It Tells Your Body To Lower Blood Sugar After You Eat.

Oxytocin

The Bonding Peptide. Your Body Releases It During Hugs And Time With People You Love.

Endorphins

Your Body's Feel-Good Chemicals. Released When You Laugh Or Exercise, To Help You Feel Good.

Research Note: The Peptides Here Are For Research Only. They Are Not Medicines. Scientists Use Them In Labs To Learn How Things Work.

What Is A Peptide Bond?

When Two Building Blocks Connect, They Grab On Tight And A Tiny Drop Of Water Pops Out. That Strong Grip Has A Name: A Peptide Bond. It Is Like Super Glue Between Two LEGO Bricks, Hard To Pull Apart.

Module 5 - Practical Basics

Handling And Storage

Peptides Are Fragile, A Bit Like A Snowflake. Heat, Light, Water, And Freezing Them Over And Over Can Break Them. So You Have To Keep Them Comfortable.

Half-Life Explorer

Tap A Peptide To See How Long It Lasts In The Body Before It Breaks Down:

Storage Guide

Temperature: 36-46 Degrees F (Refrigerator)

Dry Peptides Stay Good For 3-6 Months In The Fridge. Need Longer? Put Them In The Freezer.

Light: Keep In Original Dark Vial

Bright Light Slowly Breaks Peptides Apart. Keep Them In A Dark Bottle, Out Of The Light.

Moisture: Keep Dry Before Reconstitution

Dry Peptides Stay Safe As Long As They Stay Dry. Do Not Open Them In Steamy, Humid Air.

Freeze-Thaw: Minimize Cycles

Freezing And Thawing Over And Over Slowly Damages Peptides. Split Them Into Small One-Time Portions So You Never Thaw The Same Batch Twice.

Important: Once You Add Water To A Peptide, It Becomes Much More Fragile. Liquid Peptides Do Not Last Nearly As Long As Dry Powder.
Temperature: 36-46 Degrees F Always

Liquid Peptides Must Stay In The Fridge. Do Not Leave Them Out For More Than About Half An Hour.

Use Window: 28-30 Days Maximum

In The Fridge With Special BAC Water, A Liquid Peptide Lasts About 4 Weeks. With Plain Sterile Water, Only A Day Or Two.

Do Not Freeze After Reconstitution

Freezing A Liquid Peptide Makes Sharp Ice Crystals That Tear It Apart. For Long Storage, Keep It As Dry Powder And Only Mix What You Need.

Light Sensitivity Increases After Reconstitution

Liquid Peptides Are Even More Sensitive To Light Than Dry Ones. Keep The Bottle Wrapped Up Or In A Drawer.

BAC Water Vs. Sterile Water: BAC Water Has A Tiny Bit Of Alcohol In It That Stops Germs From Growing. That Is Why It Keeps Peptides Good For About 4 Weeks, While Plain Water Only Lasts A Day Or Two.

Module 7 - Stacking And Protocols

Stacking And Research Protocols

Stacking Means Using More Than One Peptide At The Same Time. Like Mixing LEGO Colors, Each One Does A Different Job, And Together They Can Work Better Than Either One Alone.

Why Researchers Stack

Complementary Pathways

Two Peptides Can Push The Same Goal Through Two Different Doors At Once. Using Both Gives A Bigger Result Than Either One Alone.

Some Peptides Are Quick And Are Used Right Before Sleep. Others Last For Days And Are Used Just Once A Week. Timing Them Right Makes A Difference.

Module 8 - Reconstitution Calculator

Reconstitution Calculator

Most Peptides Come As Dry Powder. To Use Them, You Add Special Water To Turn Them Into A Liquid. This Is Called Reconstitution, Which Just Means Make It Liquid Again. Getting The Strength Right Matters.

What Is Reconstitution?

Dry Powder Comes Sealed In A Little Bottle. You Add Water To Dissolve It. How Much Water You Add Decides How Strong Or Weak The Liquid Turns Out.

Reconstitution Technique

1

Let The Powder Bottle And The Water Warm Up To Room Temperature First.

2

Wipe The Bottle Top With An Alcohol Pad And Let It Dry. This Keeps Germs Out.

3

Add The Water Slowly, Letting It Run Down The Side Of The Glass, Not Straight Onto The Powder.

4

Gently Swirl Until It Looks Clear. Do Not Shake Hard. Shaking Makes Bubbles That Can Damage It, Like Shaking A Soda Can.

5

Label It With The Name And Date, Then Put It Straight In The Fridge. Use It Within About 4 Weeks.
