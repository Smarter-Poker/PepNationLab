Module 1 - Complete Overview

What Exactly Is A Peptide?

A Peptide Is A Short Chain Of Amino Acids. Think Of Amino Acids As Individual Lego Bricks. A Peptide Is What You Get When You Connect 2 To 50 Of Those Bricks In A Specific Order. When Chains Grow Beyond 50, They Become Proteins.

The Three Key Properties Of Peptides

Precision Signaling

Each Peptide Has An Exact Shape That Keys Into Specific Receptors. Your Body Does Not Confuse One Signal For Another.

Short Duration

Peptides Are Broken Down Quickly By Enzymes. Half-Lives Range From Minutes To Hours, Not Days. This Limits Off-Target Effects.

Natural Blueprint

Research Peptides Are Often Synthetic Copies Of Sequences Your Own Body Already Uses. BPC-157, For Example, Is Derived From A Gastric Protein.

Peptides You Already Know

Insulin

51 Amino Acids. Regulates Blood Glucose. Used Medically Since The 1920s. At 51 AAs, Insulin Sits At The Peptide-Protein Boundary And Is Technically Classified As A Polypeptide Hormone -- Included Here As A Well-Known Example Of A Biologically Active Amino Acid Chain.

Oxytocin

9 Amino Acids. Known As The Bonding Hormone. Naturally Released During Social Contact.

Endorphins

31 Amino Acids. Natural Pain-Relief. Released After Intense Exercise.

What Is A Peptide Bond?

When Two Amino Acids Join, The Carboxyl Group (-COOH) Of The First Reacts With The Amino Group (-NH2) Of The Second. Water (H2O) Is Released As A Byproduct. The Resulting C-N Bond Is What We Call A Peptide Bond. It Is Very Strong And Holds The Chain Together.

BPC-157 Example: BPC-157 Is A 15 Amino Acid Sequence (Gly-Glu-Pro-Pro-Pro-Gly-Lys-Pro-Ala-Asp-Asp-Ala-Gly-Leu-Val). All 14 Peptide Bonds Are Required For Its Activity.

Peptide Vs. Protein: Size Comparison

Peptide (2-50 AA)

Module 3 - How Peptides Work

The Lock And Key

Peptides Work By Binding To Specific Receptors On Cell Surfaces. Each Peptide Has A Unique 3D Shape That Fits Only Certain Receptors - Like A Key Fitting A Specific Lock. When Bound, The Receptor Sends A Signal Into The Cell, Triggering A Biological Response.

Module 5 - Practical Basics

Handling And Storage

Proper Handling Is Critical For Research Integrity. Peptides Degrade Rapidly When Exposed To Heat, Light, Moisture, And Repeated Freeze-Thaw Cycles.

Half-Life Explorer

Select A Peptide To See How Fast It Degrades In A Biological Environment:

Storage Guide

Temperature: 36-46 Degrees F (Refrigerator)

Most Lyophilized Peptides Are Stable For 3-6 Months Refrigerated. For Long-Term Storage Beyond 6 Months, Freeze At 0 Degrees F.

Light: Keep In Original Dark Vial

UV Exposure Degrades Peptide Bonds. Always Store In Amber Or Opaque Vials. Avoid Leaving Vials On The Bench Under Lab Lighting.

Moisture: Keep Dry Before Reconstitution

Lyophilized (Freeze-Dried) Peptides Are Stable When Dry. Do Not Open Vials In Humid Conditions. Use Desiccant Storage Boxes When Possible.

Freeze-Thaw: Minimize Cycles

Every Freeze-Thaw Cycle Causes Minor Structural Damage. If Using Frozen Stock, Aliquot Into Single-Use Portions Before Freezing. Do Not Refreeze A Thawed Aliquot.

Important: Once A Peptide Is Reconstituted With BAC Water Or Sterile Water, The Stability Window Changes Significantly. Reconstituted Peptides Are Far More Sensitive Than Lyophilized Powder.
Temperature: 36-46 Degrees F Always

Reconstituted Peptide Solutions Must Be Refrigerated At All Times. Do Not Leave On A Bench For More Than 30 Minutes. Use Immediately Or Refrigerate.

Use Window: 28-30 Days Maximum

Most Reconstituted Peptides Are Stable For 28-30 Days When Refrigerated With BAC Water. Sterile Water Solutions Should Be Used Within 24-72 Hours.

Do Not Freeze After Reconstitution

Freezing A Reconstituted Solution Causes Ice Crystal Formation Which Physically Damages The Peptide Structure. If You Need Long-Term Storage, Keep As Lyophilized Powder And Reconstitute Only What You Need.

Light Sensitivity Increases After Reconstitution

Peptides In Solution Are More Photosensitive Than In Powder Form. Keep Vials Wrapped In Foil Or In A Drawer When Not In Use. Work In Dim Lighting During Reconstitution Procedures.

BAC Water Vs. Sterile Water: Bacteriostatic Water (BAC Water) Contains 0.9% Benzyl Alcohol Which Inhibits Bacterial Growth. This Extends The Use Window To 28-30 Days Vs. 24-72 Hours With Sterile Water. BAC Water Is The Preferred Diluent For Research Protocols.

Module 7 - Stacking And Protocols

Stacking And Research Protocols

Stacking Means Combining Multiple Peptides In A Research Protocol To Target Complementary Pathways. The Logic Is Synergy: Each Peptide Hits A Different Receptor Or Step In A Biological Cascade.

Why Researchers Stack

Complementary Pathways

CJC-1295 (GHRH Pathway) + Ipamorelin (Ghrelin Pathway) Both Release GH But Through Different Receptors. Combining Them Produces A Greater GH Pulse Than Either Alone Because Both Pathways Are Active Simultaneously.

Sequential Mechanisms

BPC-157 (Local Tissue Effects) + TB-500 (Systemic Actin Regulation) Address Tissue Events At Different Scales. BPC-157 Acts Near The Administration Site While TB-500 Works Systemically Via Bloodstream Distribution.

Timing Optimization

Short Half-Life Peptides (Sermorelin: 15 min) Can Be Timed Relative To Sleep Onset To Align With Natural GH Pulses. Long Half-Life Peptides (CJC-1295 DAC: 6-8 Days) Are Dosed Weekly For Steady-State Research.

Common Research Protocol Frameworks

CJC-1295 DAC + Ipamorelin

Reconstitution Technique

1

Allow Both The Peptide Vial And BAC Water To Reach Room Temperature Before Reconstitution.

2

Wipe Vial Stoppers With 70% Isopropyl Alcohol. Allow To Dry Completely Before Inserting Needles.

3

Draw Calculated BAC Water Volume Into Syringe. Insert Into Peptide Vial, Direct The Stream Down The Glass Wall - Do Not Aim Directly At The Powder.

4

Gently Swirl. Do Not Shake Vigorously. Vigorous Agitation Can Cause Aggregation And Denaturation, Reducing Peptide Bioactivity. Swirl Until Clear - This May Take 30-60 Seconds.

5

Label The Vial With Peptide Name, Concentration, Date, And Initials. Refrigerate Immediately. Use Within 28-30 Days.
