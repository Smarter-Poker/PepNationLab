## PART 0 — How "Automatic" Actually Works (read first)

**What can't be automated (and why):**
- No AI (me included) can log into and post to your social accounts without your credentials — that's a hard security line, and no social-posting connector is available in this environment.
- **Fully-unattended auto-posting is unsafe for this brand.** Peptides are heavily moderated on Meta/TikTok/YouTube. A single post that reads like human-use promotion = profile ban or FTC exposure. You need one human approval checkpoint.

**The realistic automation pipeline (as close to hands-off as is safe):**
1. **I generate** a full month of platform-ready content (this document is the first batch + the template).
2. **You approve** the batch once (5–10 min — a compliance skim).
3. **A scheduler auto-posts** on the calendar. Connect the 10 accounts one time to one of:
   - **Metricool** (best for this: supports X, IG, FB, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, and bulk CSV import) — recommended.
   - **Publer** or **Buffer** (also strong; Buffer's free tier is limited).
4. **Monthly refresh** — I produce the next batch from new site content. Steady state = ~30 min of your time per month.

**The compounding trick:** every post repurposes an existing page (a monograph, a comparison, a guide, a glossary term, a calculator). So content creation is basically "reformatting what's already on the site," and every post links back — which reinforces the site's SEO at the same time.

## PART 6 — Social Compliance Rules (the guardrails that keep accounts alive)

**Always:**
- Frame as *research / in vitro / laboratory* study.
- Say "studied in [research area]" — never "for [benefit]."
- Keep "Research Use Only. Not for human or animal use." in bios and, where natural, in captions.

**Never (instant-ban / FTC risk on socials):**
- Dosing, protocols, "how to take," stacks-for-results.
- Benefit/outcome claims (fat loss, healing, anti-aging, muscle) as *effects* — only as *research areas*.
- Before/after, testimonials of human results.
- Price/discount-led promotion on TikTok/IG (triggers commerce-policy review for regulated goods).
- Medical advice of any kind.

**When in doubt:** if a post could be read as "use this compound to get [result]," rewrite it as "this compound is studied in [area] research."
